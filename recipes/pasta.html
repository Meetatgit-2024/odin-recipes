Pasta

Description

Pasta is one of the most popular italian cuisines ever.

It tastes delicious,smells delicious and looks delicious!!

You will noy stop eating it, once you start to learn how to make it.

Ingredients

  • water
  • cornflour
  • chicken breasts
  • leafy veggies
  • salt and peppers

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a large pot
  2. put the chicken into the boiling water
  3. prepare a dough from the cornflour
  4. make thin pockets from the dough
  5. add salt and pepper
  6. serve and enjoy!
